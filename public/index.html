Calendars

Show your Google Calendar events on the timeline. The planner works around them.

How to get your calendar's private link
  1. Open Google Calendar settings on the web.
  2. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click the calendar.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy Secret address in iCal format.

Anyone with that link can see the calendar, so it's only stored on this computer (in praxis.config.json). Some work accounts hide this option.
